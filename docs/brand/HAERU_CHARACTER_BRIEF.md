## What Haeru *is*

- An **original** fictional companion (Approach B): not a copy of any existing cartoon, not a depiction of a deity statue.
- **Job:** Watches over each child’s day at school; gently accompanies care updates, photos/voice moments, and progress — always **child-scoped**, calm, never ranking kids.
- **Audience age:** 2–5 (ECCD) + parents/staff who see the same character in the app.
- **Tone:** Soft, safe, warm, modern Himalayan — not cute-generic Western blue animal, not fierce dragon, not religious idol.

**One-line pitch (for parents):**  
*Haeru watches over each child’s day at school.*

**One-line for kids:**  
*Haeru brought your moments from school.*

## Visual direction (for Gemini)

### Must

- Soft, rounded forms; big friendly eyes; readable at small app sizes (favicon / avatar 64px).
- Prismatic Himalayan Rainbow palette: a luminous, pearlescent blend of lavender, dawn peach, saffron gold, Himalayan mint/teal, and sky azure (inspired by the 5 sacred Bhutanese Lungta colors and the 6 MoESD ELDS domains). Soft iridescent opal — never harsh 90s neon.
- Feels **new** — hybrid or invented creature, not “just a panda / bunny / baby dragon stock”.
- Works in: full colour hero, simple line sticker, single-colour silhouette.
- Kind guardian energy (Hariti’s *virtue*), **without** looking like a goddess, halo-heavy icon, or temple mural character.

### Must not

- Scary teeth, claws, weapons, flames, skulls.
- Hyper-anime teen / chibi idol look.
- Copying Chenrezig, Tara, Guru Rinpoche, national Druk seal, or classical Hārītī paintings.
- Logos that say “Kidu Care Pulse”.
- Crowded scenes; design **one character**, clean background or soft gradient only.

### Open look options (pick 1–2 variants in the set)

1. **Soft round guardian-friend** — abstract friendly being (no human adult body).  
2. **Tiny light-being / winged seed** — grows or glows when a school moment arrives.  

Generate **both** as separate concepts unless told otherwise.
